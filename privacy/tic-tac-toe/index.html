Privacy Policy for Tic Tac Toe

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Many Infinities handles information in the Tic Tac Toe mobile game.

Information stored locally

Tic Tac Toe is an offline-first game. It does not require account creation, sign-in, registration, or cloud synchronization.

The app stores gameplay-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Game statistics, such as wins, losses, and draws.
  • Token balance, cosmetic unlocks, and selected board theme.
  • Audio and haptic preferences.
  • Current or recently restored game-session state.

This information supports game functionality, preferences, and local progress. Many Infinities does not operate a server that receives these gameplay records.

Advertising and consent

Tic Tac Toe may use the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show advertisements. Advertising is requested only when the app is eligible to request ads under its consent flow.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent information and, where required, present consent choices. Depending on your region, consent status, and applicable Google requirements, a Privacy Choices option may appear in the app's Settings so you can revisit applicable choices.

The game remains available if advertising is unavailable or cannot be requested.

Third-party data processing

Google's advertising and consent SDKs may process information such as device and online identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, advertising interaction data, and consent-related information. The exact information, purposes, recipients, and retention can vary by region, consent choices, Google configuration, and available advertising services.

For information about Google's handling of data, review Google's Privacy Policy.

Information we do not directly collect

Based on the current app implementation, Tic Tac Toe does not directly request or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, account credentials, precise location, camera content, microphone content, or contacts.

Email contact handling

If you contact Many Infinities by email, the information you include in that message is handled through your email provider and may be retained as needed to respond to your request.

Retention, deletion, and backup limitations

Local gameplay information remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, subject to your device's operating-system behavior.

The app is configured to disable Android cloud backup and Android device-to-device transfer restoration of app data.

This configuration does not guarantee that every possible device migration, manual transfer, forensic recovery, or third-party device-management mechanism is technically impossible.

Information handled by Google is subject to Google's own retention practices and policies.

Your choices

  • Change audio and haptic settings within the app.
  • Use Privacy Choices when it is presented by the consent system.
  • Clear local app data or uninstall the app to remove local gameplay data.

Children and audience

Tic Tac Toe is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children. The app does not require accounts or knowingly request personal information directly from users.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the app, advertising configuration, consent configuration, or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.
